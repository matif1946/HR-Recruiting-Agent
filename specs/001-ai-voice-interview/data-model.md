# Data Model: AI HR Interview Screening

## Entities

### `ExperienceLevel` (Enum)
Represents the candidate's target seniority.
- `ENTRY`: Warm, encouraging, asks fundamental questions.
- `MID`: Professional, moderately challenging, scenario-based.
- `SENIOR`: Rigorous, advanced, tests technical depth and decision making.

### `VoiceState` (Enum)
Explicit visual and operational states for the voice loop.
- `IDLE`
- `LISTENING`
- `PROCESSING`
- `AI_SPEAKING`
- `ERROR`
- `COMPLETED`

### `CandidateInfo`
Personal identification and setup data for the candidate.
- `name` (string, required): Full name.
- `job_role` (string, required): Title applied for.
- `experience_level` (ExperienceLevel, required): Chosen level.

### `InterviewState`
Complete interview session metadata and context.
- `interview_id` (string/UUID): Unique identifier.
- `candidate` (CandidateInfo): Information regarding the candidate.
- `current_question_number` (int): Number of current question (1-5).
- `total_questions` (int): Set to `5`.
- `questions` (list of strings): Complete history of questions generated by the AI agent.
- `answers` (list of strings): Complete history of answers submitted by the candidate.
- `status` (VoiceState): Current session operational status.
- `created_at` (datetime): Timestamp.

### `InterviewAssessment`
Final report displayed upon completing all 5 questions.
- `candidate_name` (string)
- `applied_role` (string)
- `experience_level` (string)
- `overall_summary` (string)
- `key_strengths` (list of strings)
- `areas_for_improvement` (list of strings)
- `overall_assessment` (string)
- `disclaimer` (string): Standard disclaimer stating this is a screening aid, not a definitive hiring decision.

## Relationships & Validation Rules
- An `InterviewState` embeds a single `CandidateInfo`.
- An `InterviewState` produces an `InterviewAssessment` after exactly 5 answered questions.
- Progress updates sequentially: `current_question_number` increments strictly upon candidate answer submission.
- Once `current_question_number > total_questions`, the interview cannot accept more answers and transitions to finalized assessment mode.
